Just what to do now

MeelMore is a calm to-do list that works with your brain when the way you see time is either NOW or NOT NOW! It shows you three things to do NOW and keeps everything else out of the way until it matters, surfacing tasks at the right time and helping you get started instead of stuck.

Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone and iPad

How it works

Get it out of your head, then let MeelMore decide what's next.

Made to get you started

Small wins first, gentle reminders, and no guilt about the rest.

Plans, not just deadlines

Most dates are a plan. If one passes, MeelMore moves the task back to Not Now and asks, with no overdue pile. Real deadlines get Due and Overdue.

Already did it?

Say “I called the bank” and it lands on Now to tick off, or moves up the matching task you already had.

Work, or not work

Tap the briefcase to hide work tasks after hours, or see only work at your desk. Anything due that's hidden gets flagged.

Warm Me Up

Not ready for the big stuff? Get the three easiest tasks to build some momentum first.

Daily priorities

Each morning MeelMore asks what matters today and moves those tasks to the top.

Friendly reminders

A heads-up before something's due, worded for the task and personal to you.

Long thoughts, short tasks

Say a whole sentence. MeelMore picks out the task and the due time, and keeps the rest as notes.

Untouched tasks get smaller

If a task sits for a couple of days, MeelMore helps split it into steps you can actually start.

Search that gets it

Exact matches come first, then related tasks found by meaning, even when the words don't match.

Your order, your way

Hold and drag a task on Not Now to move it up or down. MeelMore remembers the new priority.

Works without the extras

No Apple Intelligence? Simple mode keeps everything working, just without the smart touches.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your tasks stay on your iPhone or iPad. Voice and Apple Intelligence run on the device, and nothing is sent to us. Read the privacy policy.
